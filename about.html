About Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer widely considered one of the greatest players in the history of the sport. Over more than two decades, he has played for Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al-Nassr, breaking numerous scoring and appearance records along the way. He is known not only for his technical ability but also for his extreme work ethic and physical discipline, which have allowed him to remain a top-level athlete well into his late thirties. Off the pitch, he is one of the most followed people on social media in the world.

Key dates

  1. 1985 – Born in Funchal, Madeira
  2. 2003 – Signed with Manchester United
  3. 2008 – Won his first Ballon d'Or
  4. 2009 – Transferred to Real Madrid for a then-world-record fee
  5. 2016 – Won the UEFA European Championship with Portugal
  6. 2023 – Joined Al-Nassr in Saudi Arabia

Facts

  • All-time top scorer in men's international football
  • Has won league titles in England, Spain and Italy
  • Five-time Ballon d'Or winner
  • Captain of the Portuguese national team
